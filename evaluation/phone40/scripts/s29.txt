Forty-two-year-old male with one day severe upper abdominal pain radiating to back after alcohol, plus three vomits. Denies fever and chest pain; no known drug allergy. Past history alcohol use, high triglycerides; provisional diagnosis acute pancreatitis. Examination: temperature one hundred degrees Fahrenheit, pulse one hundred and six beats per minute, blood pressure one twenty over eighty millimetres of mercury, saturation ninety-seven percent, tender epigastrium. Investigations: amylase, lipase, ultrasound. Plan: nil by mouth, IV fluids, pantoprazole forty milligrams IV, paracetamol analgesia, no alcohol. Diet: nil twenty-four hours, then clear fluids to low-fat soft diet. General: complete abstinence, return for worse pain, vomiting or breathlessness. Follow up in two days.
